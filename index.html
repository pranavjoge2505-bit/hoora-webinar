Common Questions

What Investors Usually Ask

Do I need to manage anything after investing?

No. Hoora handles recruitment, training, operations and customer service. You never need to be involved day to day.

What if order volumes are lower than projected?

Your Months 3–13 payout of ₹70,000/month (for 4 kits) is fixed and guaranteed under the signed franchise agreement, regardless of how the business performs early on. Your 4 kits sit within Hoora's wider network, so no single kit's performance determines your payout.

Is my investment amount fully transparent?

Yes. ₹11,33,000 plus 18% GST (₹13,36,000 total for 4 kits) is documented upfront, with no hidden charges or informal arrangements.

What happens after the 38-month term ends?

Renewal and continuation terms are available and will be shared as your term approaches completion.

Who actually runs the kits day to day?

Hoora-trained and managed Service Managers operate every kit to a consistent, brand-standard quality.

Is the webinar really free? Will I be pressured to invest?

Yes, it's completely free. The session is informational. You'll get the full numbers and can take your time to decide, with the option to speak 1-on-1 with the team afterwards.
